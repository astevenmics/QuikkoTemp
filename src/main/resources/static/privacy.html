Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Quikko is operated by Mystralix ("Mystralix", "we", "us", "our"), based in Texas, United States. This policy explains what information Quikko collects when you use the service, why, and for how long. If you have questions, contact us at mystralix.business@gmail.com.

There are no accounts

Quikko has no sign-up, no login, no usernames, and no profiles. You never give us your name, email address, or any other identifying information to use the service.

What we collect, and why

DataWhy we have itWhere it lives
A random anonymous session ID Generated in your browser so the server can route your matches and messages to you Browser sessionStorage only — cleared the moment you close the tab. Never written to a database.
Your IP address Read briefly to enforce rate limits and temporary bans after a report, so one person can't flood the service or keep harassing people after being reported Kept in server memory only while your connection is open. Never stored in a database, never shown to the person you're chatting with.
Interests you optionally type in Used to try to match you with someone who shares them Server memory only, discarded once your session ends.
Text chat messages Relayed in real time between you and your matched partner so the chat works Passed through, never logged or stored.
Video and audio Lets you see/hear your match Peer-to-peer (WebRTC) directly between your browser and your match's. Quikko's servers never see, store, or record it. If a direct connection can't be made, encrypted media may be relayed through a TURN server, which does not inspect or store it.
Time Capsule messages (optional) If you choose to leave a note for the stranger you just talked to, it has to be stored somewhere so it can be delivered once the 7-day window passes Stored in our database (message text, the two anonymous session IDs involved, and timestamps). Automatically deleted 90 days after it was left, whether or not it was ever claimed.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or payment information, because none of that is ever asked for.

Server logs

Our servers log minimal operational information for debugging and abuse prevention — things like a randomly generated session identifier and a note that a message was dropped for being rate-limited. We do not log chat text, video/audio, or Time Capsule content.

Cookies and similar technology

Quikko does not set tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for the full list of the strictly-necessary browser storage the site uses to function.

Advertising and analytics

Quikko does not currently run any advertising network or analytics/tracking script. The blank ad slots you may see reserve space for a future ad network and are not connected to any third party today. If that changes, we'll update this policy and our Cookie Policy first, and where the law requires it, ask for your consent before anything loads.

Other services we rely on to run Quikko

  • STUN/TURN servers — used to establish the peer-to-peer video/audio connection between you and your match. These see only the connection information needed to do that, never chat text or Time Capsule content.
  • Our hosting provider — processes your connection as part of running the service, the same way any website's host does.

Your rights

Since there's no account to delete, leaving Quikko (closing the tab) ends your session and erases the browser-side data immediately. If you left a Time Capsule and want it deleted before the automatic 90-day purge, email us at mystralix.business@gmail.com with the claim token and we'll remove it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act; contact us and we'll do our best to help, though as an anonymous service we often have no way to link a request back to a specific person beyond a capsule token.

Children's privacy

Quikko is restricted to users who are 18 years of age or older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that someone under 18 has used the service, we will take reasonable steps to delete any associated Time Capsule data.

Security

Connections to Quikko are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/WSS). We use rate limiting, input validation, and standard security headers to reduce abuse. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, note it prominently on the site.
