Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Quikko's server does not set any cookies — check for yourself, no page on this site sends a Set-Cookie header. What the site does use is a small amount of browser-local storage to make the chat itself work. This page lists exactly what's stored, why, and for how long.

Strictly-necessary browser storage

NameTypePurposeDuration
quikko_anon_id sessionStorage Your random session identifier — required for matching and messaging to work at all Until you close the tab
quikko_interests sessionStorage Remembers the interests you picked before starting a chat Until you close the tab
quikko_video_enabled sessionStorage Remembers whether you chose video or text-only mode Until you close the tab
quikko_age_confirmed sessionStorage Remembers that you already confirmed you're 18+ this session, so you're not asked twice Until you close the tab
quikko_cookie_ack localStorage Remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice banner, so it doesn't show again Until you clear your browser's site data

None of this storage is used for tracking, advertising, or analytics, and none of it is shared with anyone — it never leaves your browser except where the storage item's own purpose requires sending it (e.g. your session ID is sent to our server so it can route your chat to you).

Advertising and analytics cookies

Quikko does not currently use any advertising network, tracking pixel, or analytics tool, so there are none to list. If that ever changes, we will update this page first and, where required by law, ask for your consent through the cookie banner before anything is set.

Controlling storage yourself

You can clear this storage at any time through your browser's settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site data"), or simply by closing the tab, which clears everything except quikko_cookie_ack. Doing so won't break anything other than making you re-confirm your age and re-dismiss the cookie banner on your next visit.
